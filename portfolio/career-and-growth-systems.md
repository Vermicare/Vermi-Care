# Career, Opportunity & Growth Systems

These are not ordinary one-off job searches. They were designed as repeatable operating systems.

## 1. Career OS
**Status:** System Design / Active Use

A structured career execution system balancing:
- current-role acceleration,
- targeted international opportunities,
- long-term positioning,
- and bounded external-income experiments.

---

## 2. Global Job Search System
**Status:** Active Workflow

A recurring research and opportunity-management system covering:
- India and international markets,
- role-fit filtering,
- career-jump criteria,
- recruiter/outreach strategy,
- application tracking,
- and follow-up.

---

## 3. Freelance Opportunity Manager
**Status:** Active Workflow / Experiment

A system for finding businesses with real operational/automation problems rather than simply posting generic freelance services.

Core flow:
**problem discovery → company research → verified contact route → tailored outreach → reply classification → follow-up → qualification → scoped opportunity.**

---

## 4. Global Operations Leads Workflow
**Status:** Active Workflow / Experiment

Recurring search/outreach process focused on international businesses and Microsoft-heavy operations that may need:
- workflow automation,
- reporting,
- PMO systems,
- Power Platform work,
- or bounded subcontract support.

---

## 5. Personal Brand / Build-in-Public System
**Status:** Ongoing

Work around presenting a coherent professional identity across:
- EPMO,
- automation,
- AI,
- systems thinking,
- project engineering,
- and innovation.

The objective is to show a connected body of work rather than disconnected tools and job titles.
