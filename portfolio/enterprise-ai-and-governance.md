# Enterprise AI, EPMO & Governance Systems

## 1. CORTEX
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

An EPMO-first governance intelligence architecture combining:
- meeting intelligence,
- structured governance memory,
- decision lineage,
- RAID intelligence,
- agents,
- enterprise signals,
- controlled execution,
- and human validation.

The aim is to move governance from periodic manual reporting toward a persistent, evidence-based intelligence system.

---

## 2. Governance Meeting Intelligence Layer
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

Pipeline concept:
**Teams transcript → minutes → decisions → actions → risks/issues/dependencies → human validation → structured memory → follow-up.**

Designed to reduce the information loss that occurs between meetings and execution systems.

---

## 3. Governance Memory Graph / Governance Memory Mesh
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

Persistent representation of:
- decisions,
- risks,
- escalations,
- evidence,
- owners,
- dependencies,
- reversals,
- and historical outcomes.

The goal is organizational memory rather than treating every governance meeting as an isolated event.

---

## 4. Governance Shadow Graph
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A cross-project dependency and escalation layer designed to expose hidden relationships that ordinary project plans do not make visible.

---

## 5. Organizational Gravity Mapping
**Status:** Concept / Research

Exploration of organizational signals such as:
- meeting density,
- executive attention,
- dependency clusters,
- escalation convergence,
- communication pressure,
- and cross-functional interaction patterns.

The hypothesis is that organizational attention itself can become an operational signal.

---

## 6. Federated Planner + Central Governance Intelligence
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A Microsoft-native operating model where execution remains distributed across project teams while governance intelligence is aggregated centrally.

---

## 7. Operational Digital Twin
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A living representation of project/portfolio state built from:
- milestones,
- dependencies,
- risks,
- decisions,
- workload,
- readiness,
- and execution signals.

---

## 8. Governance Pulse Architecture
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A monitoring model for governance health and abnormal portfolio pressure, intended to identify emerging problems before conventional monthly reporting catches them.

---

## 9. Enterprise Decision Nervous System (EDNS)
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A design in which business functions emit controlled operational signals into a shared intelligence layer that supports cross-enterprise decisions.

---

## 10. Enterprise Cognitive Operating System (ECOS)
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A broader enterprise architecture exploring how an organization could:
**sense → remember → reason → predict → recommend → act → learn.**

---

## 11. Project Nervous System
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

Project-level predecessor to EDNS/ECOS. It combines delay, risk, workload, dependency, and communication signals into a live operational picture.

---

## 12. Organizational Brain / Enterprise Memory System
**Status:** Concept / Architecture

A reusable knowledge layer intended to preserve lessons, decisions, risks, solutions, and evidence across projects so organizations do not repeatedly start from zero.

---

## 13. AI Meeting Brain
**Status:** Prototype / Workflow Design

A practical Microsoft-oriented workflow:
**Teams transcript → AI-generated minutes/actions/risks → Planner/SharePoint → reporting and follow-up.**

---

## 14. MoM Approval & Distribution Automation
