# Venture & Product Explorations

A useful portfolio should include ideas that were **challenged and rejected**, not only ideas that survived.

## 1. Enterprise Decision & Action Intelligence Platform
**Status:** Archived Exploration / Market Study

Initial thesis:
create an AI layer that could continuously:
**observe → understand → predict → recommend → act → measure → learn** across enterprise operations.

Market research showed that the broad category is already heavily served by platforms such as enterprise workflow, decision-intelligence, process-intelligence, and autonomous-enterprise vendors.

**Decision:** do not pursue as a generic horizontal platform.

---

## 2. AI-native Enterprise Operations Intelligence
**Status:** Archived / Reframed

Explored as a system that could identify operational problems, recommend interventions, trigger approved actions, and learn from outcomes.

**Learning:** the architecture is valuable, but the category is too broad without a sharply differentiated wedge.

---

## 3. PMO Portfolio Intelligence Startup Wedge
**Status:** Archived / Reframed

Proposed first wedge:
AI for project/portfolio risks, dependencies, stale governance information, executive reporting, and follow-up.

Market scan found substantial existing competition.

**Decision:** do not build a generic AI PMO intelligence product without a clearly underserved problem.

---

## 4. AI Transformation Operating Layer for Microsoft-heavy Mid-market Companies
**Status:** Exploration

Reframed thesis:
help organizations already using Microsoft 365/Power Platform identify:
- what to automate,
- what is failing,
- what deserves priority,
- and whether improvements actually create value.

This remains a problem hypothesis rather than a committed startup.

---

## 5. Decision Memory / Organizational Memory Study
**Status:** Market Research

Explored whether a product could preserve:
**decision → evidence → rationale → authorization → outcome → learning**.

Research found emerging vendors already attacking decision-memory and organizational-memory problems.

**Learning:** memory alone is not sufficient differentiation.

---

## 6. Global Innovation Company Thesis
**Status:** Strategic Exploration

Long-term question:

> What problem is important enough, globally scalable enough, and personally meaningful enough to justify building an organization around it for a decade?

Current operating principle:
**problem evidence first, product second, funding later.**
