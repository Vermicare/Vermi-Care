# Personal Intelligence & Health Systems

## 1. Companion Intelligence — Life OS
**Status:** In Progress / Implemented Personal Engineering Project

A long-term Personal Operating System designed to support healthier, wiser, more autonomous decisions while preserving privacy, human agency, evidence, and transparency.

Core domains:
1. Life Operating System
2. Knowledge System
3. Reasoning Engine
4. Execution Engine (**Spider**)
5. Learning Engine

Engineering principles include:
- human autonomy over automation,
- explicit consent,
- evidence over assumptions,
- uncertainty/provenance,
- modular boundaries,
- privacy/security by design,
- no manipulation or dark patterns.

Active private repository: `Vermicare/companion-intelligence-life-os`.
