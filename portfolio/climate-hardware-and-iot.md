# Climate-Tech, Hardware & IoT

## 1. Adaptive Non-AC Personal Cooling System
**Status:** Prototype / Documented R&D

A personal-cooling project that began with the question:

> Can a person be cooled efficiently without refrigerating an entire room?

The early design explored chilling and recirculating water through cooling media. It evolved away from a conventional AC architecture toward localized thermal comfort.

Key directions explored:
- micro-hydronic heat exchange,
- directed airflow,
- PCM thermal buffering,
- humidity-aware operation,
- dry comfort air separated from moisture/heat-rejection air,
- radiant heat suppression,
- condensation/dew-point protection,
- adaptive sensing and control.

## 7. Dual-Zone Atmospheric Cocoon
**Status:** Concept

This was intended to avoid the humidity penalty of direct evaporative cooling.
